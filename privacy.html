Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Draft for the firm's review. This policy is a starting point written in plain language. The firm should review and adjust it, including registration with the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner if required, before relying on it.

1. Who we are

Abdiwakil & Associates Advocates ("we", "us") is a law firm at Fort Granite Flats, 2nd Floor, Suite B3, Block B, Bishop Road, Upperhill, Nairobi, P.O. Box 42429-00100. We are responsible for the personal data collected through this website. You can reach us at abdiwakil@gmail.com or +254 722 696225.

2. What we collect

When you use the enquiry form we collect your name, email address, phone number (if you provide it), the area of law you select, and the message you write. If you contact us on WhatsApp, we see your phone number and the messages you send.

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. Our hosting provider and the services listed below may process technical data, such as your IP address, to deliver the site.

3. Why we use it

  • To respond to your enquiry and arrange a consultation.
  • To take steps you ask us to take before we act for you.
  • To meet our legal and professional obligations.

We rely on your consent when you tick the box on the form, and on taking steps at your request before a contract with us. We do not use your details for marketing unless you separately ask us to.

4. Who we share it with

We do not sell your data. We use these service providers to run the site:

  • Formspree, which receives enquiry form submissions and forwards them to us.
  • Netlify, which hosts this website.
  • Google Fonts and Google Maps, which load fonts and the map on the contact section.
  • WhatsApp (Meta), if you choose to message us there.

Some of these providers are based outside Kenya, so your data may be processed abroad. Please do not send passwords, payment card details or other highly sensitive information through the form.

5. How long we keep it

We keep enquiries only as long as needed to deal with them and to meet our professional record-keeping duties. If you become a client, our file retention rules apply to your matter.

6. Your rights

Under the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019, you may ask us to confirm what personal data we hold about you, to correct it, to delete it, or to stop using it, and you may withdraw consent at any time. Contact us using the details above. You also have the right to complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner of Kenya.

7. Confidentiality

We treat enquiries as confidential. Submitting the form or messaging us does not by itself create an advocate-client relationship; that begins only when we confirm that we are acting for you.

8. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
