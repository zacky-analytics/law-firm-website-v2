About the firm

A Nairobi law firm built on straight talk and careful work

Abdiwakil & Associates Advocates serves individuals, businesses and organisations across Kenya. We explain your options in plain language, tell you what to expect, and keep you informed at every stage.

Abdiwakil Hujale is the founding and managing partner and an Advocate of the High Court of Kenya. He advises on corporate governance, conveyancing and dispute resolution, and appears for clients before the courts, regulatory bodies and mediation panels in Nairobi and beyond.

  • Confidential. What you tell us stays with us.
  • Plain language. No jargon without an explanation.
  • Responsive. We reply to enquiries promptly, including on WhatsApp.
  • Practical. We focus on the outcome you need, not on paperwork for its own sake.

Practice areas

How we can help

From buying land safely to resolving a dispute in court, these are the matters we handle most.

Land & Property

Title searches and verification, sale agreements, transfers, subdivisions, leases and land disputes.

Commercial & Business

Company registration, partnership agreements, contracts, regulatory compliance and commercial disputes.

Civil Litigation

Representation in court for debt and asset recovery, personal injury and insurance claims, and contract disputes.

Employment Law

Employment contracts, wrongful dismissal, workplace disputes and advice for both employers and employees.

Notary Public

Notarisation and authentication of documents so they are accepted locally and abroad.

Commissioner for Oaths

Witnessing and certification of affidavits, sworn statements and statutory declarations.

Legal Consultation

One-to-one advice on a property purchase, a business decision or a dispute before it becomes a court case.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

How do I book a consultation?

Call or WhatsApp +254 722 696225, or send the enquiry form on this page. We will get back to you to confirm a time.

What should I bring to the first meeting?

Your national ID or passport and any documents connected to your matter, such as title documents, agreements, letters, court papers or receipts. If you are not sure what is relevant, bring what you have and we will go through it together.

Is my enquiry confidential?

Yes. We treat everything you share with us in confidence. See our Privacy Policy for how we handle information submitted through this website.

Do you handle matters outside Nairobi?

Yes. Our office is in Upperhill, Nairobi, and we act for clients in other parts of Kenya as well. Contact us with the details and we will advise.

Can you notarise or commission documents?

Yes. We provide notary public and commissioner for oaths services. Please contact us before you come in so we can confirm what you need to bring.
