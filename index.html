Our Profile

Abdiwakil & Associates Advocates is a respected law firm based in Upperhill, Nairobi. Our mission is to provide trustworthy, professional, and results‑driven legal services for individuals, businesses, and organizations across Kenya.

With years of experience in diverse legal fields, our firm stands on the principles of integrity, excellence, and commitment to justice. We believe that every client deserves personalized attention, clear communication, and strong representation.

Abdiwakil Hujale

Managing Partner / Advocate of the High Court of Kenya

As the founding and managing partner of Abdiwakil & Associates, Advocate Abdiwakil leads the firm's strategic legal consultations, bringing a robust wealth of experience in corporate governance, conveyancing, and dispute resolution.

He has actively represented clients across diverse regulatory bodies, high courts, and mediation tables in Nairobi and beyond, structuring the firm around the core values of uncompromising integrity and dedicated client advocacy.
